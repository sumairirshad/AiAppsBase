Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What's included in the tour price?+

Accommodation, listed meals, local guides, ground transport and entrance fees are included. International flights are booked separately so you can use your own points or airline.

Can I customize a group itinerary?+

Yes — our travel designers can tailor any itinerary for private groups of 6 or more. Reach out after booking to start customizing.

What is your cancellation policy?+

Free rescheduling up to 14 days before departure. Cancellations made 30+ days ahead receive a full refund minus a small booking fee.

Do I need travel insurance?+

Yes, comprehensive travel insurance is required for all international itineraries and can be added during checkout.
