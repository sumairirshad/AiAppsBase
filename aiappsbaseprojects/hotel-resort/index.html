What time is check-in / check-out?+

Check-in from 3:00 PM, check-out by 12:00 PM. Early arrival and late departure can be arranged subject to availability.

Is breakfast included in the rate?+

All suite and villa rates include daily breakfast at Sunrise Terrace. Half and full board options are available.

What is your cancellation policy?+

Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. Within 7 days, one night's rate applies.
