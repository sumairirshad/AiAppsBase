What's the typical project budget range?+

Full residential projects typically start around $45,000 including furnishing. Single-room consultations start at $600.

Yes — we take on select projects nationally, with on-site visits scheduled around key milestones.

Can you work with our existing contractor?+

Absolutely. We regularly collaborate with clients' preferred contractors and can also recommend trusted partners.
