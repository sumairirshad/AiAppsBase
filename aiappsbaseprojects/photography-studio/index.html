Behind the Lens

Marcus Lind

I started Lumen Studio in a one-room apartment with a single speedlight and a lot of stubbornness. A decade later, the tools have changed — the obsession with light hasn't.

My work has been featured in Vogue Italia, Kinfolk and The New York Times, but the sessions I'm proudest of are the quiet, personal ones most people never see.

— Marcus

Good to Know

How far in advance should I book?+

Portrait sessions: 2–3 weeks. Weddings and commercial campaigns: 2–6 months depending on season.

Do you travel for shoots?+

Yes, worldwide. Travel fees apply outside the NYC metro area and are quoted upfront.

How do I receive my photos?+

Through a private online gallery where you can view, download and order prints.
